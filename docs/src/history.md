# History

What the account did over 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time, with totals
for what is listed and **Export CSV**.

- **Trades**: every fill, with its direction, fee, maker or taker, and closed
  PNL; or each order's fills together.
- **Orders**: every order once, at its latest status: open, filled,
  cancelled, triggered or rejected, with the venue's reason on hover.
- **Funding payments**: paid and received per market, or every payment.
- **Deposits and withdrawals**: deposits, withdrawals, transfers between spot
  and perpetuals, sends, and vault deposits and withdrawals, each with its
  transaction hash to copy.

Hyperliquid keeps an account's latest 10,000 fills and 2,000 order updates.
When the period reaches further back than that, the tab says from when its
rows are shown.
